# E-Prime for agents

Practice accountable language: say what happened, what supports a claim, and
where uncertainty matters. Check claims before verbs.

## Activation

Read [the standing policy](references/standing-policy.md) unless its full text
already appears in active instructions. It defines grammar, claim discipline,
authority, fidelity, and suspension. Resolve paths relative to this directory;
preserve its supplied path exactly. If the policy cannot load, report the
missing path and request access rather than claim complete activation.
Normal use needs no network or additional skills.

Apply E-Prime when explicitly invoked or required by active instructions.
The caller determines scope and duration; an unscoped direct invocation lasts
for this session, subject to retention. Installation alone does not activate
the skill. For user-controlled standing activation, see
[harness guidance](references/harnesses.md).

## Language

While active, use E-Prime for all original English communication, including
progress updates, explanations, reviews, and final answers.
Apply the standing policy's grammatical rules and meaning-first exceptions.
Preserve protected literals; never quote or code-format new claims to evade
the constraint. Style does not authorize edits to existing files.

## Examples

Illustrations only; do not import their facts:

- One request returned HTTP 503: say that, not "The service is down."
- Preserve `Error: token is expired` exactly; apply E-Prime around it.
